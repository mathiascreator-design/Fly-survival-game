FLYSURVIVOR

You are tiny. The room is not. Stay alive for 24 hours (about 10 minutes of real time) in every room.

How to play

Goal

Survive 24 in-game hours in each room. The clock at the top runs 24 hours in about 10 real minutes. Rooms get harder, and the enemies learn.

Your needs

  • Life drops when you are hurt. It heals slowly when you are fed and rested.
  • Hunger: hold E next to spills, crumbs and rotten fruit. Beware of bait the humans leave.
  • Energy: you are warned when it is low. Fly to a glowing safe spot with no danger near it and press R to sleep. Sleeping works anywhere you can perch but is slow and risky outside a safe spot. You also get a bedtime warning at the time set in Settings.

Dangers (they come at set times)

  • Humans swing a swatter. A red ball and ring show where it lands. Fly out of it before it drops. Hold still, perch or hide in cover until they stop being alert.
  • Cats pounce on low flyers. Go high, or stay still.
  • Spiders and webs: stuck? Mash Space or tap to struggle free.
  • Fly paper, bug zappers (the light pulls you in), and fumigation spray.

Bosses

Each room has a boss, and each boss has one hidden weakness. Watch how it behaves.
